Social media platforms are designed to keep us engaged, often at the expense of our attention and sleep routines. Most digital wellbeing tools respond by setting limits or blocking access. Comeback explores a different approach.

We redesign the consumption experience through three mechanisms.

First, circadian-aware friction introduces grayscale and unpredictable interruptions during a configured nighttime window.

Second, an AI-powered active-recall gate turns access to entertainment into an opportunity to retrieve knowledge from the user's own study notes.

Third, logarithmic rewards encourage screen disengagement without allowing rewards to grow linearly with time.

A virtual clock lets us demonstrate these mechanisms across the day-night cycle in a controlled environment.

Comeback is currently a prototype, not a clinically validated treatment for digital addiction. Our next step is to test whether these interventions actually change usage patterns and improve users' ability to disengage.

Our vision is to move digital wellbeing beyond simply restricting technology and toward designing technology that respects human attention.
